Open your project and choose Copy brief. Paste it into ChatGPT or Codex, and attach the relevant files there. The instructions define responsibilities, handoffs and quality checks.

Your AI session performs the work using available tools. This dashboard does not run background agents, access your files, or track their progress automatically. Update project status yourself and export your briefs as backups.
